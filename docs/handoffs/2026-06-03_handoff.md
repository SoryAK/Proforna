## Last Completed Step
Two layout bugs diagnosed and fixed:

**1. Blank screen (critical)**
- **Root cause**: `<AIChat />` was a direct child of the root `flex-col h-screen` div. AIChat has `h-[100dvh]` (md+: `position: relative`). In a `flex-col`, height is the main axis — `h-[100dvh]` consumed all flex space, leaving the inner `flex-row` with zero height → blank white screen (only AppHeader rendered).
- **Fix**: Moved `<AIChat />` inside the `<div className="flex flex-1 overflow-hidden">` row. In `flex-row`, height is the cross-axis; `h-[100dvh]` is safely clipped by `overflow-hidden`.
- **File**: `src/components/layout-shell.tsx`

**2. Scrollbar visually bleeding under the header**
- **Root cause**: AppHeader's `<header>` had `bg-transparent` and no `border-b`. On the right edge of the viewport, the header area and main content area both showed `bg-gray-50` (root div background). No visual separator — scrollbar track started at y=64px with no visible boundary, making it appear to bleed behind/under the header.
- **Fix**: Changed header classes from `bg-transparent` (no border) → `bg-background border-b border-border shrink-0`.
- **File**: `src/components/sidebar.tsx`

**Commit**: `c402180` on `main` — "fix: layout blank screen + scrollbar-under-header"
