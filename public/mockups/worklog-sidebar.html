Worklog Sidebar — Pattern X vs Pattern Z

Both mocks: folders + categories + library moved into the global sidebar. Activity widget moves to top of notes-list (your option a). The worklog page itself becomes two panes: notes-list + editor.

Pattern X — Swap

Sidebar contents replace top-level nav while in worklog

Single sidebar width. Click "Worklog" → sidebar swaps to worklog nav. Click "← Back" or another top-level via a hamburger to return. Trade-off: top-level nav is hidden while you work.
