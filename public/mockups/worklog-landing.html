Design notes

  • Hero = Quick Capture. The single most-used action is "I want to log a thought right now." It owns the top of the page.
  • Today section is the inline reading surface — full text of recent notes, tappable to expand into the editor. No empty pane staring at you.
  • Stat cards are click-through: streak → opens 12-week heatmap modal; this-month → goes to filtered list; notable → goes to notable list. Same callbacks as today's stats strip, just bigger surfaces.
  • Folders grid replaces the rail's "Folders" group as a click-through entry. Sidebar still has the full folder tree for quick switching while you're working.
  • "View all notes" goes to /worklog/notes — that's where the existing 2-pane list+reader lives. The detailed scan-and-edit view is preserved, just relocated.
  • Sidebar stays the override version we just shipped; only "Home" is now an active row alongside All notes / Notable / Templates.
  • Notable star, category dot color, time-ago all reuse existing tokens — nothing new from tokens.md.

Open questions for you

  1. Does the "Today" inline cards section feel right, or do you want a tighter one-liner list there?
  2. Routing: /worklog = home, /worklog/notes = current 2-pane — OK or different shape?
  3. Quick Capture: stay inline (Save adds today's entry), or open into a modal/full editor on Save?
