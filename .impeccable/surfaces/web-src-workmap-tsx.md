# Work Map surface brief

Scope: `web/src/WorkMap.tsx` and its role editor, map, and publication settings.
Mode: Operate.

Audience: an occupant maintaining a private, detailed career record. The job
is to explore roles across time and geography, enrich a selected role, and
publish only when the map is ready. Career roles, sites, milestones, work
conditions, equipment, media, privacy settings, and publication state are the
real content. Resume Studio remains separate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career ledger and geographic map are one authoring instrument.
The surface refuses a dashboard of interchangeable cards and keeps editing
connected to the role's place and chronology.

OWN-WORLD: Extend Proforna's established near-black operating surface, fine
gold rules, restrained filled actions, upright Geist type, and compact
workhorse controls. Real map tiles provide the visual ground.

STORY: The occupant first sees that this is a private Work Map, scans the role
ledger, selects or places a role, enriches its evidence, and only then chooses
the disclosure settings for an immutable relay snapshot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Identity and private state sit above a split ledger and map.
Roles remain visible beside geography. Selecting a role opens a focused editor
over the map; map placement temporarily returns the map to the foreground.
Publication is explicit but visually secondary to enrichment.

FORM: Established-world operating-surface extension, first and only applicable
form. Seed key: `n/a-extension`; the Impeccable contract explicitly skips a
concept roll for a local extension inside an established surface.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
