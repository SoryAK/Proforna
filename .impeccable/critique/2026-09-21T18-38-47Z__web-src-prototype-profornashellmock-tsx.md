## Priority Issues

**[P0] The phone layout cannot be used**
- **Why it matters:** At a phone width the three columns stack into short clipped strips. The name clips, Sessions crush the thread, and the composer only peeks in.
- **Fix:** One primary surface on small screens. The other two become sheets. Do not equal-split three panes.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

**[P0] Two primary surfaces cancel each other**
- **Why it matters:** The center is labeled Career file and contains an avatar and a name. The only guidance lives in the side conversation. An occupant cannot tell which pane is the work.
- **Fix:** Put a real Career document in the middle, with status an occupant can act on. Keep Proforna docked and subordinate until it is opened.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

**[P1] Ask and the model name sit in the Operate chrome**
- **Why it matters:** `Ask`, `qwen3.8:27b · Local`, and `Proforna · qwen3.8:27b` read as agent controls. You already chose that composer on the live panel, so this is a conflict with “talk to Proforna,” not an accident in the mock.
- **Fix:** Keep one model chip if you still want it. Attribute replies as Proforna. Do not repeat the model name in the thread label and the toolbar.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P1] The explorer asks for too many choices at once**
- **Why it matters:** Seven destinations, four always-open jobs, and three sessions are on screen together. The job rows all open the same placeholder, so the tree looks like navigation and is not.
- **Fix:** Four top destinations. Nest Resources and roles. Show Sessions when the conversation is the focus, not as a second nav above the thread.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable quieter

**[P2] The DEV switcher and the title chip shout over the ledger**
- **Why it matters:** The cream pill and three title strings (Proforna, Home — Career file, DEV mock) compete with the document.
- **Fix:** Leave the switcher as throwaway chrome. Shorten the title chip to the brand and one status.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish
