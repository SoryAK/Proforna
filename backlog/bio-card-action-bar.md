# Bio card action bar

The Publish, Preview, and Edit controls on the Career History bio card need a better place.

## Problem

They sit as plain text on one line across the top of the bio card, with Home, History, and the Revoked status. That row does not look right. The words compete with the name and read as a label strip rather than actions.

## Direction

Pop Publish, Preview, and Edit out into their own action pill bar. The reference is a bar of extra action overlay buttons: a separate control, not a line of text inside the card. Keep the same actions. Find the placement when this is picked up. Do not restyle the job card pills as part of this.
