# Duplicate review entry

Review duplicates cannot be reached from the sidebar.

## Problem

The Review duplicates button lives on the `#/history-reserve` page. The Career History link opens a different screen without it. When an import brings in a copy of a role, there is no visible way to compare, merge, or mark it as a different role.

## Direction

Decide where duplicate review belongs on the Career History the sidebar opens, and show its entry there when there is something to review. Keep the review itself as it is.
